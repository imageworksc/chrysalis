What Can We Help With?

Mental health concerns can look different from person to person. Chrysalis Health provides individualized care for a wide range of emotional, behavioral, and family challenges.

  • Anxiety & Excessive Worry

    Support for persistent worry, fear, panic, avoidance, and anxiety that begins interfering with everyday life.

  • Depression & Withdrawal

    Help with sadness, irritability, low motivation, isolation, hopelessness, and loss of interest in everyday activities.

  • Trauma & Difficult Experiences

    Trauma-informed care to help people process difficult experiences, understand their reactions, and develop healthier ways to cope.

  • Anger & Behavioral Concerns

    Support for emotional outbursts, aggression, difficulty managing strong emotions, and behavioral challenges.

  • Child & Teen Concerns

    Mental health support for children and adolescents experiencing emotional, behavioral, school, social, or family difficulties.

  • Family Challenges

    Help navigating conflict, communication problems, separation, divorce, major life changes, and other challenges affecting the family.

What mental health services does Chrysalis Health provide?

Chrysalis Health provides individual therapy, child and teen therapy, family therapy, anxiety and depression therapy, trauma therapy, anger management, psychiatric services and medication management, case management, and telehealth. Services are individualized based on each person's needs and availability in their area.

How We Can Help

Different people need different kinds of support. Care begins by understanding what you are experiencing and identifying the services that may best fit your needs.

  • Therapy

    Individual, child and teen, family, anxiety and depression, trauma, and anger-management therapy.

  • Psychiatric Services & Medication Management

    Psychiatric evaluation and ongoing medication management when clinically appropriate.

  • Case Management

    Support coordinating care, services, resources, and other needs that may affect treatment and overall wellbeing.

  • Telehealth

    Secure remote mental health services that can make care easier to access when travel, distance, schedules, or mobility create barriers.

Care for Children, Adults & Families

  • Children & Teens

    Age-appropriate therapy and mental health support built around a child's development, symptoms, strengths, family, school, and environment.

  • Adults

    Individualized mental health care for emotional, behavioral, relationship, and life challenges that may be affecting everyday wellbeing.

  • Families

    Support that helps families better understand one another, improve communication, work through conflict, and navigate difficult changes together.
